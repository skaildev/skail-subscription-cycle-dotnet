Subscription cycle

One skail function runs the whole life of a subscription: a free trial, then one charge a month, a retry when the card is declined, and the end when the customer cancels, the card fails twice or the 12 months are over. Between the charges it waits with durable delays, which hold no thread or process. In the demo the trial lasts 20 seconds and a month lasts 10.

You play the customer:

  1. Pick the card the customer has, and click Start trial.
  2. Subscribe to a plan during the 20-second trial, or do nothing and let the trial end.
  3. Watch a charge a month; cancel whenever you like.
