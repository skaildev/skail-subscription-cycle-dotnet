Subscription cycle

A skail function runs the whole life of a subscription: a 20-second trial, then one charge every 10 seconds (a month, in the demo). A declined charge gets one retry 5 seconds later, and a second decline suspends the subscription. A cancellation stops the charges, and after 12 months the cycle ends.
